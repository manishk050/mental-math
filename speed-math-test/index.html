Speed Math Test

Test your mental calculation speed with exactly 10 questions covering addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. The timer measures your elapsed solve time while the score keeps track of correct answers and wrong attempts.

Start the speed math test

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard before starting. For a focused drill, select one operation; for a broader test, select several.

What makes this a speed test?

The test is designed around a simple balance: answer correctly, but try to reduce the time you spend thinking about familiar calculations. There is no countdown and no penalty for taking a moment to correct a mistake.

10 questionsEvery completed test has exactly 10 problems.
Accuracy requiredWrong answers keep you on the same question until you solve it.
Elapsed-time scoreYour final time measures how quickly you completed all 10 correct answers.

How to use the test

1. Pick your operations

Select addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, or any combination. The test distributes the selected operations across the round.

2. Choose a difficulty

Easy uses smaller numbers, Medium expands the range, and Hard uses larger arithmetic. Division problems are generated with whole-number answers.

3. Type and submit

You can type numbers without clicking into an answer box. Press Enter to check the answer and Backspace to edit it.

4. Review your result

When all 10 answers are correct, the test shows your total time, correct answers, wrong attempts, and chosen difficulty.

How to improve your speed

Practice the operation that causes the most hesitation instead of only repeating mixed tests. Once common addition and multiplication facts are automatic, spend more time on larger calculations that require decomposition.

Also track mistakes. A fast time with several wrong attempts can hide the real difficulty. A better benchmark is a time you can repeat while keeping the error count low.

Frequently asked questions

Is the test free?

Yes. The speed math test runs in your browser and does not require an account.

How many questions are there?

Exactly 10 questions per round.

Does a wrong answer move to the next problem?

No. The same question remains active until the correct answer is entered.

Can I practice just one operation?

Yes. Select only that operation before starting.
